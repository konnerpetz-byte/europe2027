ii.

One language, three accents

All three countries speak German, but the hello changes as you cross each border. These are the ones worth having ready.

The tell: In Bavaria & Austria you'll hear Grüß Gott (formal) or Servus (casual, also means "bye"). On the trail, Austrians say Griaß di. Cross into Switzerland and it becomes Grüezi. Using the local one earns a smile.
